# Product selection guidance

- Light shelf capacities are read directly from `assets/orcamento.json` (`Tabuleiro ... até ...Kg`) so commercial guidance follows the configurator catalogue. Limits are per shelf and evenly distributed, not total bay capacity.
- Medium cantilever: manufacturer EvoRacks, single-access technical sheet, downloaded 2026-11-07: https://evoracks.es/index.php?controller=attachment&id_attachment=44 . Its table explicitly labels CASF0840–CASF1060 values as load per pair of arms (275/250/225/250/225/200 kg). Corrected the former equipment-weight column in both languages.
- Tyre counts are illustrative geometric estimates with stated usable length and tyre width; they are not confirmed configuration capacities.
- Fork dimensions, wheel material, runtime, locker lock options and workbench load limits remain subject to confirmation for the supplied variant. No numerical values were inferred from generic equipment images or battery capacity.
