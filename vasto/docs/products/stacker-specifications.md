# Stacker — verification of product information

Checked 2026-11-07. Applies to the JMAO unit shown in the existing product photographs.

## Direct evidence (takes precedence over online family catalogues)

- `public/produtos/2-Stacker/a3.jpg`: equipment plate, model PSL15-30; capacity 1500 kg; maximum lift 3 m; working voltage 48 V; motor power 1.0 kW; overall size 1.82 × 0.82 × 2.04 m. Model punctuation normalised for display. Do not interpret motor power as the sum of traction and pump motors.
- `public/produtos/2-Stacker/a4.jpg`: four LEOCH 6-EVF-40 batteries, each labelled 12 V / 40 Ah / 20 hr. Emergency-stop button, key switch and hydraulic unit visible. Do not add the four Ah ratings together. The site states the per-battery specification rather than assuming an independently verified pack rating.
- `public/produtos/2-Stacker/a5.jpg`, `a6.jpg`, `Image.png`: JMAO branding; walk-behind tiller; telescopic mast, lifting chains, protective mesh, forks and load wheels.

## Primary web sources consulted

- LEOCH EVF range, https://www.leoch.cn/product/power/99.html — exact 6-EVF-40 reference, lead-acid type, 12 V / 40 Ah. Other component dimensions and battery weight are not stated as equipment specifications.
- Chinlift PSL15/20 family, https://www.chinliftforklift.com/product/electric-stacker-psl1520 — supports electric walk-behind architecture and 48 V family. Not established as the manufacturer or exact JMAO variant; do not import its 490 kg weight, 1788 mm length, fork dimensions, motor types, speeds or options into this unit's specifications.

Other PSL15-30 listings also vary (e.g. 550 kg, 2050 mm lowered mast). They are not evidence for this JMAO unit.
